bysound with a backend

A payment request where only a 12-byte fingerprint travels by sound. The amount, the merchant and who published it come from the backend (server.mjs, running locally).

  1. Sender: the shop publishes a request on the backend, then plays only its fingerprint.
  2. Receiver: the customer resolves the fingerprint on the backend, sees the verified publisher and the amount, and confirms.
  3. Rogue: an attacker nearby plays a different fingerprint at the same time.

Open each page in its own window (not a background tab: browsers slow down timers in hidden tabs), or on different devices against the same backend. Speaker volume high. See README.md for what to watch.
