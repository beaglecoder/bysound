The automatic channel ladder

With band: 'auto' (the default of sendSession) the sender starts on the least audible channel and steps down top → high → upper → mid, three repetitions per channel, until the receiver acknowledges. After six normal repetitions it goes robust. The ack says where it was read, and that is remembered for the next send.

1. Simulation (no microphone needed)

Every repetition is rendered with encode, passed through a filter and some microphone noise, and read with offline decode. Turn on the broken tweeter and watch the ladder converge on mid, the lowest channel. A model, not a measurement of a real speaker.

#sent onprofileread on

2. Live, between two devices

Open this page on a second device, press Listen there, then Send here. The log shows each repetition's channel; "delivered" comes from the ack and says where the other side read it.
